Why we're here

A world worth returning to.

Heartwood Online Forever began with an appreciation for Heartwood's world and the friendships built there. This independent fan project is an effort to create another place for that community to gather.

Development currently focuses on the essentials: accounts, characters, exploration, combat, gathering, crafting, and multiplayer. These systems are being rebuilt and tested before public access opens.

Built from a love of the world. Shaped by the people who play it.

The aim is a reliable, welcoming experience, developed at a sustainable pace. Community feedback will help guide priorities. Features, scope, and release plans may change as testing progresses.

— Founder, Heartwood Online Forever

What's coming

Familiar adventures. A careful rebuild.

The world you remember

Explore familiar places as world interactions and server systems are rebuilt. Stability and gameplay are still being tested.

Core systems first

Combat, inventory, quests, and multiplayer are under active development, with fixes checked through testing in the game client.

Room to grow

Additional quests, items, areas, and events are longer-term goals. The immediate priority is getting the foundations ready for players.

Built with the community

Share ideas, report issues, and follow development in Discord. Feedback from future playtests will help shape the next steps.

Open communication

The commitment is to share progress and communicate changes to the plan. There is no fixed release or update schedule yet.

Free to play

The project is planned as a free-to-play experience. Public access details will be shared when testing is ready.
