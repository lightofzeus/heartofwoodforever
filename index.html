Why we're here

A world worth returning to.

Heartwood Online Forever began with an appreciation for Heartwood's world and the friendships built there. This independent fan project gives that community a place to gather again.

The open beta covers the essentials: accounts, characters, exploration, combat, gathering, crafting, and playing together. The server is live, and the rebuild continues in the open, with fixes shipped as players find problems.

Built from a love of the world. Shaped by the people who play it.

The aim is a reliable, welcoming experience, developed at a sustainable pace. Player feedback sets the priorities, so if you want something changed, added, or fixed, say so in Discord. Features, scope, and plans may still change as the beta goes on.

— Founder, Heartwood Online Forever
